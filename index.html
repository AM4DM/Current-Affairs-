Complete State Bills, Legislative Acts & Govt Schemes

1. Uttarakhand Land Law (Amendment) Act 2025

Passed Assembly Feb 2025
  • Complete Ban on Agricultural Land Sale: Outsiders/non-residents prohibited from purchasing agricultural or horticultural land in 11 hill districts.
  • Residential Land Limit: Cap of 250 sq. meters strictly enforced for residential purposes in urban hill limits.
  • Affidavit & Background Check: Mandatory District Magistrate verification, police clearance, and sworn affidavit verifying purpose before any land transaction.

2. Uttarakhand Minority Educational Institutions Authority Act 2026

Effective July 1, 2026
  • Abolition of Madarsa Board: Uttarakhand State Madarsa Board completely dissolved.
  • Unified Minority Authority: Replaced by single umbrella authority covering Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Jain, Buddhist, and Parsi institutions.
  • Curriculum Mandate: Mandatory introduction of NCERT syllabus, modern science, computer education, and state audit controls.

3. Aroma Revolution Policy (2026 – 2036)

10-Year Framework

Aims to establish 7 specialized Aroma Valleys (Damask Rose Valley in Chamoli, Lemongrass Valley in Haridwar/Udham Singh Nagar, Cinnamon, Lavender, Mint) targeting ₹1,200 Cr market ecosystem by 2036.
